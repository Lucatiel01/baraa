Who We Are

مخيم البراء — Al-Bara'a Camp is home to over 756 displaced people living in Gaza. We are farmers, teachers, artisans, and students — a community that refuses to be defined by displacement. Despite limited resources, we run a tight-knit community. Our goal is simple: survive with dignity and build a future for our children while we wait for peace.

Urgent Needs

These are our most critical shortages right now. Any support — large or small — makes a difference.

Medical Supplies

Our clinic is out of basic antibiotics, painkillers, and chronic-disease medication. 1 in 3 children has not received routine vaccinations.

Winter Tents & Blankets

Winter temperatures drop below freezing. Over 200 families lack proper thermal blankets or waterproof tent sheeting.

Education Materials

Our school serves 400+ children but lacks textbooks, notebooks, and basic classroom supplies. Teachers work without salaries.

Clean Water Infrastructure

The camp's water trucking is inconsistent. A solar-powered well and filtration system would serve the entire camp.

Psychosocial Support

War and displacement have taken a heavy toll. We need trained counselors and safe spaces for children and women.
